## What's new

- Console, Users & billing: changing a user's package or role and deactivating a user now ask for confirmation, QuickBooks Disconnect and Run test receipt ask first, and the sign-in method column names its provider.
- Console, QuickBooks: receipts deleted in QuickBooks are labelled "deleted in QuickBooks" instead of "failed".
- Console, Email Reports: the monthly report is sent on the last day of the month (February included) and the yearly one on December 31st; content toggles confirm that they saved.
- Console, Search Console: Avg Position is weighted by impressions, countries show their names, indexing states read as words and the device and country charts say they count clicks.
- Console, Analytics and Marketing agency: an empty Analytics card explains itself, error and warning text is readable in the light theme, the campaign form fields are labelled and the disabled "Write the campaign" button says what is missing.
- Console: each page has its own browser tab title, the Costs plan filter shows capitalised plan names, and the Go-to-market plan keeps its own item numbers (91. is no longer shown as 1.).
