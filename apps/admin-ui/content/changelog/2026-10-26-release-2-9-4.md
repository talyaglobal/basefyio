## What changed

- The platform version is a three-digit counter where each digit runs from 0 to 9: 2.9.0 → 2.9.1 … 2.9.9 → 3.0.0. Today's three releases, published as 2.10.0, 2.10.1 and 2.10.2 for a few hours, are now 2.9.1, 2.9.2 and 2.9.3; their release notes and tags carry the new numbers.
