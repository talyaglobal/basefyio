## Fixed

- **No import widget over the login form.** After a Supabase import was started and the page was left, the small "Connecting to Supabase" or "Importing" box in the bottom-right corner could come back on the sign-in page and flicker. A saved import is now picked up again only when you are signed in, and right after you sign in. Imports older than 12 hours are forgotten.
