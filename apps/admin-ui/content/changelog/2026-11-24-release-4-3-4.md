## Fixed

- **Deleted projects stay in the history.** When a project was deleted for good, the record of why it was deleted went with it. Now a record is kept every time a project is moved to the trash, restored, or deleted for good, including when the 24-hour trash period runs out. The **Project deletion reasons** list in the console shows what happened to each project and puts the newest first. This applies to deletions from now on. Projects deleted for good before this release are not in the list.
- **"No reason given" is its own answer.** If nobody picks a reason, the record now says "No reason given" instead of "None of the above". The reason dialogs no longer preselect an answer.
- **Read a trashed project's activity.** While a project is in the trash, team members can open **View activity** on its card to see who did what. The console project page shows the same history and the project's real name, without the internal "_deleted" ending.
- **The Overview deleted count is right.** Preview branches are no longer counted as projects, and projects deleted for good are counted separately.
