## What's fixed

- **Extra database roles now log in through the pooler.** A login role created under **Extra database roles** was rejected with "password authentication failed" on `db.basefyio.com:6432`, while the same role worked on a direct connection. The pooler's credential list held the role's stored (encrypted) password instead of the real one. It now holds the real password, so no change is needed on your side: roles created before this release work as soon as the pooler reloads its list, which happens at the release. Your passwords did not change and were not exposed.
