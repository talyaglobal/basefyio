## What's new

- **Sign-in asks for your authenticator code.** If you turned on an authenticator app for your basefyio account, the sign-in page now asks for the 6-digit code after your password. Before, an account with an authenticator could not sign in with a password at all.
- **Organizations can require two-factor authentication, and it is enforced.** When an owner turns the requirement on, a member who has not set it up is sent to the setup page at their next sign-in, whether they sign in with a password, Google or GitHub, and gets no session until it is done. The CLI login is refused the same way until setup is done. The owner must have two-factor authentication on before turning the requirement on, so they cannot lock themselves out. Sessions that are already open stay signed in.
- Nothing changes for accounts without an authenticator in organizations that do not require one.
