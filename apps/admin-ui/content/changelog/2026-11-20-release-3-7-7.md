## What's new

- **The basefyio AI assistant is part of the Pro plan and above.** Ask, plan and agent modes answer only for teams on Pro, Business or Enterprise. Teams on Free get a message with the upgrade path instead of an answer.
- **The right team's plan counts.** The plan of the project you have open is used, otherwise your active team's plan.
- Embeddings and retrieval over your own project documents are not affected.
