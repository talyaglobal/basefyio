## What's new

- **Guided tours are rolling out.** Over the next days each dashboard page gets a short tour that points out its main buttons and says in one sentence what each one does. A tour starts by itself the first time you open a page.
- **Replay any time.** The Tour button in the top bar shows the tour of the page you are on again.
- **Your choice follows your account.** Tick Don't show again to stop automatic tours on every device. You can turn them back on, or show every tour again, under Account, Guided tours.
