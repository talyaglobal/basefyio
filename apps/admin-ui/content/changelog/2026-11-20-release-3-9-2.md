## What's new

- **Point-in-time restore is a background job.** Starting a restore answers `202` right away with a job id. Progress is available from `GET /api/projects/:id/pitr/restore/:jobId` and `GET /api/projects/:id/pitr/status` (queued, running, succeeded or failed, with a plain-language message).
- **The dashboard follows the restore step by step.** The dialog shows progress, the queue position and any failure, and picks an active restore back up after a reload. Closing it does not stop the restore.
- **Restores cannot starve the platform.** One restore per project at a time, one running restore across the platform, a short queue, a per-project hourly limit and a 60-minute time limit. The scratch database is memory- and CPU-capped.
- **A failed restore leaves your database as it was.** The final load runs in a single transaction and rolls back on any error. Failure messages say whether the database was changed.
- Self-hosters can pause new restores with `PITR_RESTORE_ENABLED=false` and tune the limits; see the configuration reference.
