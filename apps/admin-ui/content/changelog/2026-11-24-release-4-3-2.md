## What's new

- **Fewer background requests.** The dashboard used to load every page behind a visible link in the background, about 36 requests each time `/dashboard` opened. Sidebar links now load their page when you point at them or tab onto them, and the dashboard cards and shortcuts load theirs when you click. The first click on a card can take a moment longer, in exchange for a lighter and quieter page.
