## What's new

- **Pay with PayPal:** an unpaid invoice now offers **Pay with PayPal** next to **Pay now** once PayPal is enabled for your platform. You approve the payment on PayPal and return to Billing, where the invoice turns Paid.
- **Terms at sign-up:** creating an account requires accepting the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy and the Data Notice (KVKK). They now state that your account details may be used for promotional communications about basefyio, with an opt-out at support@talyasmart.com.
