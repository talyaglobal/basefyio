## What's new

- Console: the Overview and Costs pages now show the same recurring revenue (the list price of every paying plan), and say so.
- Console: the Stripe tab lists only subscriptions, charges, invoices and customers that belong to a team, shows the team and the plan name, and counts what it left out.
- Sheet to App: name your blueprint before it is analyzed, each blueprint has its own page address, deleting shows a confirmation toast, the target project list is searchable, and a starter Excel template can be downloaded.
