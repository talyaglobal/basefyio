## What's new

- The Team and Billing pages name the team they show; with All Teams selected they say so and point to the header menu.
- A pending invite no longer lingers for someone who is already a member, and Re-invite and Cancel invite only appear for roles allowed to invite.
- Cancelling a team-name edit puts the saved name back. The invite form checks the email address before it enables Send Invite.
- Members who are not owners can leave a team from the Team page; owners transfer ownership first.
- The rename note on the Team page now matches the role permissions table, and the icon buttons have labels for screen readers.
