## What's new

- **Signed in until sign-out.** Leave **Token expiry (seconds)** empty under **Auth → Settings** and your app's users stay signed in until they sign out. Their session does not run out. Access tokens are still short-lived, one hour, and basefyio-js renews them in the background, so signing out or revoking a session still ends it at once. Through the API, send `tokenExpirySeconds` as `null`, `""` or `0`.
- **No 30-day limit.** A token expiry of 300 seconds or more is accepted, longer than 30 days too. The sign-in session lasts at least 30 days, or as long as the token if that is longer.
