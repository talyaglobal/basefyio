## What's new

- **The AI assistant writes valid PostgreSQL.** Plan and Agent answers are now told to use PostgreSQL only, to express ownership with the platform's `auth.uid()`, `auth.role()`, `auth.jwt()` and `auth.email()` functions, and to follow the policy clause rules (`INSERT` takes only `WITH CHECK`, `UPDATE` takes both). It no longer invents a `users` table or claims a change was applied.
- **The AI assistant understands Turkish.** Valid Turkish requests, and English ones that were wrongly turned away, are accepted. Clearly off-topic or abusive input is still refused.
- **Row-level security: a better owner-column default.** The policy editor picks a user or owner column of type uuid or text instead of the first column, and asks you to choose when nothing fits, with a warning if the column is not uuid or text.
- **The database advisor explains itself.** Its reasons follow your answers, search needs suggest enabling embeddings, and step 2 shows the suggested starter and its tables. Projects are still created empty.
- **Easier to read in the light theme.** Muted text and the console sidebar labels have stronger contrast.
- **The Trash purge tooltip is readable.** It had collapsed to a sliver.
- **Docs work on a phone.** A navigation drawer with search, scrollable tables, and a header that fits a 390px screen.
