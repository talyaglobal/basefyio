## What's new

- Bell notifications are now saved to your account instead of living only in the open tab.
- The list and the unread badge load when you open the dashboard and stay the same after a reload.
- Clicking a notification, or opening its browser alert, marks it as read for good.
- "Mark all read" now clears the unread badge on every device.
