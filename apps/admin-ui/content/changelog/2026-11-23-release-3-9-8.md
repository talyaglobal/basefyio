## What's new

- **The home page no longer promises a trial.** It mentioned a 14-day and a 30-day free trial, but plans have no trial period: Free is free to start with no credit card, and Pro and above are billed monthly with a card on file. The hero and the plan cards now say exactly that, and the paid plans' button reads "Choose Pro" instead of "Start trial".
