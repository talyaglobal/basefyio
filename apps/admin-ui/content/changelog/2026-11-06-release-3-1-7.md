## What's new

- A signed-in user's access token is accepted again by `/rest/v1`, `/auth/v1/user` and the other project APIs. It was refused with `401` (`bad_jwt`, `PGRST301`) even though sign-in and refresh worked.
- Uploading raw bytes (a `Buffer` or `Uint8Array`) with `storage.from(bucket).upload(...)` works again instead of answering `400`. Blob and multipart uploads were not affected.
