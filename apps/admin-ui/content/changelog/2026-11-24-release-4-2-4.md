## What's new

- **Several memberships per role.** An extra database role can now be a member of `anon` and `authenticated` together, or of any mix of the built-in roles: send `memberOf` as a list. The role gets none of their rights until its session switches to one with `SET ROLE`.
- **Per-role time limits that stay.** `statementTimeout`, `idleInTransactionSessionTimeout` and `lockTimeout` can be set on each role, for example `"30s"` or `"5min"`. Before, these settings were removed every time the role was repaired; now they are part of the role and are applied again each time.
- **Connect through the pooler.** Extra roles can now log in through the connection pooler on port 6432 with their own password, not only directly.
- **Change a role later.** `PATCH /api/projects/:id/db-extra-roles/:roleId` updates the memberships or limits of an existing role. The extra roles API is now documented.
