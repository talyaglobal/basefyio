## What's new

- **Change or cancel from your current plan.** Under Available Plans, your current plan has a red **Cancel** button. It opens a dialog with your options: upgrade to a bigger plan, switch to a cheaper paid plan, or downgrade to Free.
- **Switch to a cheaper paid plan, for example Business to Pro.** You keep the plan you paid for until the end of the period, and nothing is charged or refunded today. When the period ends, your plan changes and the new price is charged. The billing page shows the date, and you can undo the switch until then. Before you switch, any unpaid balance must be paid and your usage must fit the new plan's limits.
- **Pay now after a failed payment.** When a payment fails, the warning on the billing page now has a **Pay now** button. It charges your saved card at once, next to the option to update the card.
