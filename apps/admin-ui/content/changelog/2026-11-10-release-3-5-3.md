## What's new

- Auth > Email: the template preview now renders like a real e-mail, dark text on a white page, in dark mode too.
- Overview: the "By Status" chart tooltip and bar highlight are readable in dark mode.
- Feedback: comments keep their line breaks, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends, Reply jumps to the comment box, adding a comment shows a toast, and the attachment viewer offers Open in new tab and Download.
- Feedback: small files show their real size (KB instead of "0.0 MB"), and the remove buttons on picked files have accessible labels.
