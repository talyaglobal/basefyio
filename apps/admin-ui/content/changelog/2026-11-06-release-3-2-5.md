## What's new

- On phones the project menu is a drawer opened from a Menu button, and the AI assistant starts closed and opens as an overlay, so project pages use the full screen width.
- Below 1280 px the top bar collapses into the menu button (it now also lists Changelog and Docs); the theme toggle and notifications stay in the bar at every width.

## What's fixed

- The top bar no longer overflows at 1024 px and 768 px; the notifications bell and user menu are always reachable.
- Dates and numbers use one English format everywhere ("Oct 21, 2026", "1,000") instead of mixing Turkish month names, day-first dates and dotted thousands. Times show your time zone. The card form on Billing is always in English.
