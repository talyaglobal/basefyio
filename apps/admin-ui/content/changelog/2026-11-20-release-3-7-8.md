## What's new

- **Several cards per organization.** Payment Method lists every saved card with a Default badge; add a card, make another one the default, or remove one.
- **Paid plans keep at least one card.** The last card on Pro, Business or any paid plan cannot be removed. Legacy and grandfathered accounts are exempt and may remove all cards. A Free organization can remove its last card unless money is owed.
- **Upgrading needs a card.** Choosing Pro or above asks for a card first, then continues the upgrade.
- **Moving to Free is guided.** The dialog pays what is owed with the default card, checks projects, members, storage and database size against the Free limits (and lists what to reduce), then schedules the switch for the end of the paid period. Monthly API, bandwidth and active-user figures only show as warnings. Nothing is deleted automatically.
- The Billing page no longer shows the organization name next to the figures.
- Existing subscriptions, plans and saved cards are unchanged; the new rules apply only when someone removes a card, picks a plan or moves to Free.
