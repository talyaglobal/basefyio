## What's new

- Deleting a folder or a tag on the Projects page now opens a confirmation that shows how many projects it holds. The projects themselves are always kept.
- Clicking All Projects or a folder while the Trash or Deactivated view is open now takes you to that list instead of staying put.
- The What's New card sits below dialogs, so it no longer covers a modal or its backdrop.
