## What's new

- When someone comments on your feedback, edits it or changes its status, you get one email and a live notification in the dashboard, with a link straight to the item. A comment and a status change made together arrive as a single update, and you are never notified about your own actions.
- Feedback you left in Done is closed automatically 3 days later, and you are told so. Reply to reopen it. You can still close it yourself at any time with Close Ticket.
